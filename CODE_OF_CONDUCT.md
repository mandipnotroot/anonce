# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

Audience: everyone participating in project spaces or representing the project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity,
rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including
race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity,
disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation,
language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position,
level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation are
extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this
Covenant.

## Encouraged behaviors

Social norms and interpretations differ across cultures, backgrounds, and languages.
We agree to behave mindfully toward each other, including:

- Respecting the purpose of our community, its activities, and its ways of gathering.
- Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
- Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
- Supporting the well-being of our community.

## Restricted behaviors

Instances, threats, and promotion of these behaviors violate this Code of Conduct:

- Harassment, including violating expressed boundaries or continuing unwanted attention.
- Character attacks, demeaning comments, stereotyping, or discrimination.
- Inappropriately intimate or sexualized behavior in a community context.
- Sharing or acting on private information without permission.
- Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
- Other behavior threatening community well-being.
- Impersonation or misleading identity to evade enforcement.
- Failing to credit the sources of contributed content.
- Promotional or commercial content outside community norms.
- Irresponsible communication that includes or promotes restricted behavior.

## Reporting an issue

- Mandip moderates project spaces. No dedicated confidential reporting address is published.
- Request a private channel through the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/mandipnotroot).
  Do not disclose incident details publicly, even if no private channel is available.
  GitHub's reporting mechanisms remain available for platform abuse.
- Moderators take reports seriously and aim to respond promptly. They may review
  relevant messages, logs, recordings, and witness accounts.
- Confidentiality and safety take priority. Enforcement is normally private;
  an agreed resolution may include a public statement.

## Addressing and repairing harm

Responses depend on the severity, impact, repetition, and prospects for repair.
Moderators may use any of the following remedies without a mandatory ordering:

| Remedy | Application and possible repair |
| --- | --- |
| Written warning | Explain the harmful behavior, required boundaries, and opportunities for apology or clarification. |
| Temporarily limited activity | Restrict specific interactions or channels with a defined cooldown and return conditions. |
| Temporary suspension | Remove participation for a defined period with clear conditions for reintegration. |
| Permanent ban | Remove access when continued participation cannot be reconciled with community safety. |

Remedies should have clear reasons and be proportionate. Appeals may be requested privately.
If the sole maintainer is involved, seek an independent moderator available and
agreeable to affected parties. One maintainer cannot promise independent adjudication.

## Scope

This policy applies in all community spaces and when an individual officially
represents the community, including through an official account, address, appointed
role, or event appearance.
