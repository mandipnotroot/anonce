Intended boundaries

The identity side verifies membership. A separately deployed submission service is intended to receive anonymous authorization and ciphertext, never membership credentials or plaintext answers.

Members and reviewers must retain their private keys on their own devices. This build has no key generation, import, signing, encryption, or decryption operations.

Important limits

The intended design cannot protect compromised devices, identifying answers, all operator collusion, or global traffic observation. It is not a coercion-resistant voting system.

Loopback binding is not authentication. Other users and processes on this computer may reach the local services. Do not expose them through an external proxy.

Review the design and implementation

The corresponding source includes the roadmap, threat model, protocol design, and evidence requirements. A source link is not independent verification of the code delivered by a server.
