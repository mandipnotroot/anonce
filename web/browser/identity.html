Account

Passkeys authenticate your account. They do not unlock encrypted keys. There is no account or key recovery in this MVP.

Private account access

Your browser automatically creates and encrypts the member key. No app, CLI, key files or certificate requests are needed. Choose a strong, separate local unlock passphrase. It never leaves this browser. Lost passphrases cannot be reset. Restoring on a new browser trusts the service's public-key binding; malicious-service substitution and rollback protection are not provided.

Active account sessions

At most 16 sessions may be active. Times describe authentication and expiry, not devices or anonymous activity. Reauthentication replaces the current session without extending its expiry.

Manage sign-ins

    Removing other sessions requires passkey authentication within five minutes. Revocation blocks new server actions, but cannot recall admitted work or erase keys and text already loaded elsewhere. Open pages check sign-in when foregrounded and every 30 seconds while visible and running. Offline or suspended pages cannot observe remote revocation. Encrypted backups and membership are unchanged.

    Authorize one response

    Authorize only this common review. This consumes your sole allowance even if signing or delivery is interrupted. It does not send an answer or account identifier to the anonymous service.
