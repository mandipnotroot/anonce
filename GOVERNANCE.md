# Governance

Audience: contributors, adopters, and prospective maintainers.

## Maintainer

Mandip ([mandipnotroot](https://github.com/mandipnotroot)) maintains the repository,
reviews changes, approves releases, and administers repository permissions. The
commit address is a noreply address, not a contact channel.

## Decisions

Use issues for problems and proposals and PRs for changes. Update the relevant
[reference document](docs/README.md) when a requirement changes. Seek agreement
through public technical discussion. The maintainer resolves disagreements with a written
rationale, particularly where security assumptions or compatibility are affected.
Private vulnerability details must stay in the disclosure channel until coordinated
publication is appropriate.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Review accepted changes and take responsibility for their contents.
- Keep security claims aligned with implemented and tested behavior.
- Preserve licensing, source availability, and contributor credit.
- Review dependency provenance and publication permissions.
- Document material governance and security decisions.

Additional maintainers may be appointed after sustained review and maintenance work,
with agreement on disclosure handling and release access. Repository permissions
must be limited to the responsibilities actually held.

## Release and financial authority

Only a maintainer may approve a release or change publication credentials. There is
no project treasury, paid support commitment, or service-level guarantee. Sponsorship
does not confer technical decision authority.

## Conflicts and continuity

Declare relevant conflicts of interest. Ask an uninvolved reviewer to assess a
contested change where possible. Maintainers may transfer stewardship only with
public notice and a documented access handover. The license permits independent
forks; no project policy restricts that right.

Conduct enforcement and appeals are defined in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
