# Governance

Audience: contributors, adopters, and prospective maintainers.

## Maintainer

Mandip ([mandipnotroot](https://github.com/mandipnotroot)) maintains the repository,
reviews changes, approves releases, and manages permissions. The noreply commit
address is not a contact channel.

## Decisions

- Use issues for problems/proposals and PRs for changes. Update the
  [owning reference](docs/README.md) when requirements change.
- Seek agreement publicly. The maintainer resolves disagreements with written
  rationale, especially for security or compatibility decisions.
- Keep vulnerability details private until coordinated publication is appropriate.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Review accepted changes and take responsibility for their contents.
- Keep security claims aligned with implemented and tested behavior.
- Preserve licensing, source availability, and contributor credit.
- Review dependency provenance and publication permissions.
- Document material governance and security decisions.

New maintainers may be appointed after sustained review/maintenance and agreement
on disclosure and release access. Limit permissions to actual responsibilities.

## Release and financial authority

- Only maintainers may approve releases or change publication credentials.
- No project treasury, paid support commitment, or service-level guarantee.
- Sponsorship gives no technical decision authority.

## Conflicts and continuity

- Declare conflicts; seek an uninvolved reviewer for contested changes where possible.
- Stewardship transfers require public notice and documented access handover.
- The license permits independent forks; project policy does not restrict that right.

Conduct enforcement and appeals are defined in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
